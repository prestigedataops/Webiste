Four services. One goal: time back.

Each service works on its own or together. We start with an audit, design the system with you, build it, and keep improving it.

01

Workflow Automation

Hand-offs, approvals and data entry, running without anyone touching them.

Best forTeams losing hours to repetitive admin and copy-paste between tools.

What it does

  • Maps your current process and finds the steps worth automating first.
  • Automates approvals, hand-offs, notifications and data entry.
  • Connects the tools you already use instead of replacing them.
  • Adds checks and alerts so nothing slips through.
  • Shows you what ran, and what needs a person.

Works alongside

  • Email
  • Spreadsheets
  • CRM
  • Accounting
  • Calendars

02

AI Agents & Chatbots

Agents that answer, qualify and act, trained on your own knowledge.

Best forTeams answering the same questions or handling inbound requests all day.

What it does

  • Answers customer and staff questions from your own documents and data.
  • Qualifies leads and books meetings, jobs or appointments.
  • Takes actions in your tools, not just conversation.
  • Hands off to a person when it should, with full context.
  • Improves over time from real conversations.

Works alongside

  • Website chat
  • Phone
  • Email
  • Messaging

03

CRM & Tool Integrations

One source of truth. Your tools finally share the same data.

Best forBusinesses with data scattered across disconnected tools.

What it does

  • Syncs customers, deals and jobs between systems.
  • Removes duplicate entry and conflicting records.
  • Builds dashboards from combined data.
  • Finds another route when a tool has no API, or tells you plainly.

Works alongside

  • CRM
  • Helpdesk
  • Accounting
  • Calendars
  • Databases

04

Custom Internal Tools

Dashboards and portals built around how your team actually works.

Best forTeams working around spreadsheets and off-the-shelf software that does not fit.

What it does

  • Builds dashboards, portals and simple apps for your workflows.
  • Gives staff the exact views and actions they need.
  • Includes permissions and audit trails.
  • Hosted and monitored by us, owned by you.

Works alongside

  • Your databases
  • Existing software
  • Reporting
