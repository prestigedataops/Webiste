Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what Prestige DataOps ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website only.

What we collect

If you contact us or request a free audit, we collect what you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number and the message you write. We do not ask for more than we need to reply.

Like most websites, the servers that deliver this site may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and, if you ask, arrange an audit call.
  • To keep records of our business conversations.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

How your message reaches us

The contact form either opens your own email app with your message ready to send, or sends it to us through a form service we use for this purpose. You can always email us directly instead.

Third parties

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads. We share your enquiry details only with providers that help us run the website and reply to you (for example hosting and email), and when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set its own cookies or use advertising trackers. If we add analytics later, we will update this page first.

Phone calls handled by an AI Voice Agent

This policy does not cover calls to our clients' businesses. If we build an AI Voice Agent for a business, that business decides how its calls are handled, and the terms agreed with that business apply.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to reply and to keep reasonable business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing contact@prestigedataops.com.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@prestigedataops.com.
