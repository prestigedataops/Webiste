01

AI Call Answering & Booking

Every call answered around the clock, in a natural voice and without a phone tree.

Best forCompanies missing calls after hours, in peak season, or while the office is already on the line.

What it does

  • Answers new and existing customer calls 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.
  • Triages no-heat, no-cool, leaks and routine requests using your rules.
  • Escalates safety issues such as a gas smell or carbon monoxide alarm to a person immediately.
  • Books appointments onto your schedule with the details your tech needs.
  • Transfers to your team during business hours, or takes a message and alerts the on-call person after hours.
  • Announces call recording at the start of the call where required.

Works alongside

  • Phone system
  • Field-service software
  • Calendar
  • Text messaging

02

Lead & Quote Follow-up

The first reply wins the job. Every new lead and open quote gets a fast, polite response.

Best forCompanies whose web, Local Services or marketplace leads wait for the office to catch up.

What it does

  • Replies within minutes to web forms, Google Local Services and marketplace leads.
  • Asks the right questions and offers booking times.
  • Follows up on quotes and estimates nobody has answered, and stops once the customer replies or books.
  • Flags warm leads to your comfort advisor or sales lead.
  • Contacts only people who have reached out to you or opted in.

Works alongside

  • Web forms
  • Local Services leads
  • CRM
  • Text and email

03

Maintenance Agreement Automation

Recurring revenue stays recurring when renewals and tune-ups run themselves.

Best forCompanies with a customer list that is not being worked every season.

What it does

  • Sends renewal reminders ahead of expiry, with an easy way to confirm.
  • Runs spring and fall tune-up campaigns to agreement customers.
  • Reactivates lapsed agreements and past customers.
  • Books tune-ups straight onto your schedule.
  • Shows which renewals are confirmed, pending or at risk.

Works alongside

  • Customer database
  • Field-service software
  • Text and email
  • Calendar

04

Reviews & Reputation

More genuine reviews from happy customers, and quick action when something went wrong.

Best forCompanies whose online rating does not reflect the quality of their work.

What it does

  • Asks every customer for feedback after a completed job, by text or email.
  • Makes it easy to leave a public review in one tap.
  • Alerts you immediately when a customer reports a problem so you can make it right.
  • Drafts replies to reviews for your approval.
  • Tracks review volume and rating over time.

Works alongside

  • Google Business Profile
  • Field-service software
  • Text and email

05

Back-Office Automation

Stop retyping the same job details into three different systems.

Best forOffices where dispatchers and admins spend hours on data entry.

What it does

  • Attaches call summaries and notes to the right customer record.
  • Syncs job details between your phones, field-service software and accounting.
  • Sends invoice and payment reminders on your schedule.
  • Produces daily and weekly summaries of calls, bookings and open items.
  • Flags missing information before it reaches the technician.

Works alongside

  • Field-service software
  • Accounting
  • Phone system
  • Reporting
